# Use demand-driven bounded affiliate pipeline replenishment

The affiliate pipeline uses downstream pull rather than a daily discovery quota. Every fifteen minutes the gateway reconciles discovery, capture, intake, queue, lease, and retry state. The Mapping Queue targets two waiting jobs per active Mapping Producer, while the Review Queue permits two waiting jobs per active Independent Supply Reviewer. Active claims do not consume those waiting slots. Review pressure pauses new Mapping claims and campaign replenishment; a missing reviewer pauses both.

When Mapping work falls below target and downstream capacity exists, the gateway runs one replenishment wave for the highest-priority eligible campaign or requests one Coverage Planning Job. The wave stays active until its discovery and capture results become terminal or materialize Mapping Jobs. Valid overshoot is retained, another wave starts only after reconciliation, and provider failure never counts as zero Marginal Yield. Search Saturation review dates are earliest eligibility dates rather than execution deadlines.

A versioned Replenishment Demand opens immediately when Fresh Published Supply falls below its Supply Target and closes only when that same contract target is restored. Discovery remains behind refresh, regression repair, activation, review, and already-captured mapping work. Priority propagates through every handoff. Immutable operational events and gateway receipts support fifteen-minute rollups, a read-only operations dashboard, and source, wave, lane, or global fault containment without imposing queue-age service-level promises.
