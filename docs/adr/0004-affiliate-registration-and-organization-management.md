# Define Affiliate Events by External Registration, not Event Type

Affiliate Event is a commercial context equivalent to External Registration and independent of Event Type, Event Provenance, Management Authority, and Resource Source. Organization-created, platform-sourced, and unclaimed Affiliate Events can use External Registration with outbound click tracking.

Scope revision, 2026-09-05: External Registration and destination-domain changes do not require Tracking Pixel integration or verification. Tracking Pixel integration, its domain verification, and purchase attribution are deferred to later work. Outbound click tracking remains in scope. Outbound clicks do not prove completed registrations or purchases.

Organization Claim makes the Organization the Management Authority and initially assigns its owner as Event Host for every Organization Event without changing Registration Destination or Schedule. Switching to BracketIQ registration removes the Affiliate Event classification but preserves competition, Resources, Schedule, provenance, authority, and historical attribution.
