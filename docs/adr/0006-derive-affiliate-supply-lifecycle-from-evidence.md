# Derive the affiliate Supply Source lifecycle from evidence

A Supply Source is rooted in one source-intake identity and links its discovery, capture, Mapping Job, live source, mapping, Organization, runs, candidates, and public targets. Its current Mapped, Approved, Activated, or Published stage is derived by one `affiliateSupplyLifecycle` module from authoritative evidence rather than stored as a mutable stage field. Immutable lifecycle transitions record authority, generation, active Supply Contract, reason codes, and evidence; rebuildable projections support queues and reporting without becoming a second source of truth.

Approval creates reversible Approved Supply. Governed activation atomically validates the reviewed generation, prepares and publishes at least one valid target and the appropriate Organization, stores the reviewed baseline, validates the mapping, and enables recurring imports last. A healthy source with no current target is Activated Supply; a qualifying target makes it Published Supply. Natural expiry returns Published Supply to Activated without disabling automation, while drift or an automation hold returns it to Approved. Fresh Published Supply alone contributes to versioned Supply Targets; retained Last-Known-Good targets remain visible but count zero.

Machine-verified same-origin canonical URL changes preserve the lifecycle root and require revalidation. Cross-origin or operator changes create a linked successor and transfer no approval or automation authority. Source Exclusion does not hide existing targets, and Published Target Rejection affects only the evidenced target. Existing public records survive legacy reconciliation; historical approval, baseline, or scrape evidence is never fabricated.
